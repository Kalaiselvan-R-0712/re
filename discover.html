The discovery loop

From question to confidence.

A simple rhythm for turning something you notice into something you understand, practise and use.

01

Question

Start with something you genuinely want to understand or solve.

02

Explore

Collect perspectives, examples and explanations that make the subject clearer.

03

Experiment

Put the idea into practice through a project, challenge or real-world task.

04

Reflect

Notice what changed, strengthen what you learned and choose the next question.
